# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With QA Lead:** Collaborate on test case design, implement acceptance tests, receive early feedback
- **With Technical Architect:** Follow architectural patterns, participate in design reviews, propose technical solutions
- **With Product Manager:** Clarify acceptance criteria, discuss implementation trade-offs
- **With Project Manager:** Provide updates on progress, flag blockers and dependencies

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Project Manager:** Align on scope, timeline, and stakeholder expectations
- **With Developers:** Define acceptance criteria, discuss feasibility and trade-offs
- **With QA Lead:** Validate user workflows, confirm feature completeness
- **With Technical Architect:** Understand technical constraints and capabilities
- **With Design/UX Lead:** Collaborate on user experience and design standards
- **With Stakeholder/Sponsor:** Report on progress toward business outcomes

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Product Manager:** Align on priorities and scope changes
- **With Developers:** Monitor progress, identify and escalate blockers
- **With QA Lead:** Report quality metrics, coordinate release readiness
- **With Technical Architect:** Understand and track technical risks and dependencies
- **With Release Manager:** Coordinate release planning and deployment schedules
- **With Stakeholder/Sponsor:** Escalate critical decisions and risks

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define quality standards, design test strategies, and validate that features meet acceptance criteria before release. They collaborate with Product and Development teams to ensure quality is built in, not bolted on.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality criteria and test strategies for each project
- Design and maintain test plans, test cases, and automation frameworks
- Validate acceptance criteria before features move to Done
- Identify and triage quality risks
- Collaborate on Definition of Done standards
- Conduct release readiness assessments and smoke testing
- Track and report quality metrics

### Goals
- Ensure all releases meet quality and security standards
- Reduce post-release defects and customer escalations
- Build quality awareness across the team
- Enable faster feedback loops through automation

### Typical Communication
- Participation in sprint planning and backlog refinement
- Test plan reviews with product and engineering
- Daily standups and QA status updates
- Release readiness sign-off
- Quality metrics and trend reports

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Developers:** Collaborate on test case design, automate acceptance tests, provide early feedback on implementation
- **With Product Managers:** Refine acceptance criteria, validate user workflows, confirm feature completeness
- **With Project Managers:** Report quality metrics, identify quality risks for escalation, coordinate release readiness
- **With Release Manager:** Conduct smoke testing, validate deployment procedures, confirm post-deployment functionality
- **With Technical Architect:** Define performance and security testing requirements

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects make strategic technology decisions, evaluate design trade-offs, and ensure projects align with enterprise standards, scalability, and security requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design reviews and approve architecture proposals
- Evaluate technology choices (frameworks, platforms, infrastructure)
- Define scalability, performance, and security requirements
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers on architectural patterns and best practices
- Ensure alignment with enterprise standards and compliance requirements

### Goals
- Ensure systems are scalable, maintainable, and secure
- Reduce technical debt and architectural rework
- Accelerate development through clear architectural guidance
- Enable consistent, reusable patterns across projects

### Typical Communication
- Technical design document reviews
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Participation in technical spike planning
- Escalation of technical risks in project syncs
- Mentoring sessions with development team

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Developers:** Review designs, approve technology choices, provide mentorship on implementation patterns
- **With Project Managers:** Flag technical dependencies and risks, estimate effort for architectural work
- **With Product Managers:** Discuss trade-offs between features and technical constraints, inform product roadmap with technical capabilities
- **With QA Lead:** Define performance and security testing requirements
- **With Release Manager:** Review deployment architecture and rollback procedures

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors represent business interests, provide funding authority, and set strategic direction. They are the escalation point for organizational or business-level decisions and ensure projects align with business strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project initiation and resource allocation
- Align project objectives with business strategy
- Provide final decision authority on scope trade-offs
- Remove organizational blockers
- Receive and approve executive status updates
- Champion project outcomes and drive adoption

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Maintain strategic alignment across initiatives
- Minimize organizational friction
- Enable successful project delivery and adoption

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or quarterly executive updates
- Decision approval on scope changes or resource requests
- Escalation reviews for high-impact risks
- Milestone celebrations and launch announcements
- Stakeholder steering committee meetings (as needed)

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Project Managers:** Provide governance and escalation path for major decisions, approve resource requests
- **With Product Managers:** Align on business outcomes and strategic priorities, validate customer impact
- **With Teams:** Occasionally attend milestone demos to understand progress and impact, provide strategic guidance

## Scrum Master / Delivery Coordinator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team blockers, and maintain consistent delivery cadence. They serve as the connective tissue between the team and management, ensuring the team can focus on delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove team impediments and blockers
- Maintain sprint board and delivery metrics
- Coordinate sprint logistics (scheduling, tools, communication)
- Coach team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track velocity and burn-down metrics
- Communicate team health and escalate systemic issues

### Goals
- Enable consistent, predictable delivery
- Foster team cohesion and psychological safety
- Reduce time spent in meetings and maximize focus time
- Build a culture of continuous improvement

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Impediment tracking and escalation
- Velocity and metrics reporting
- One-on-one coaching and feedback
- Team retrospective facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Developers:** Remove blockers, protect focus time, coach on agile practices
- **With Project Manager:** Provide delivery metrics and team health signals, coordinate cross-functional dependencies
- **With Product Manager:** Manage backlog refinement sessions, facilitate prioritization workshops
- **With QA Lead:** Coordinate QA activities within sprint, ensure test execution keeps pace with development
- **With All Roles:** Foster collaboration and psychological safety

## Design / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design/UX Leads own the user experience strategy, define design systems, and ensure all customer-facing products meet usability standards and brand guidelines.

### Responsibilities
- Define user experience strategy and design principles
- Create and maintain design systems and component libraries
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Design interfaces and interactions that meet accessibility standards
- Review and approve UI/UX implementations
- Collaborate with product on feature design and prioritization
- Mentor designers and maintain design quality standards

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible products that users love
- Ensure consistent experience across products and platforms
- Reduce user support burden through better design
- Enable faster design-to-development handoff

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions with product and engineering
- User research findings and recommendations
- Design system updates and component guidance
- Accessibility audit results and remediation plans
- Design critique and feedback on implementations

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Product Manager:** Collaborate on feature design, validate user research, iterate based on metrics
- **With Developers:** Provide design specifications, review implementations, assist with design system integration
- **With Stakeholders:** Present user research findings, advocate for user needs in priority discussions
- **With QA Lead:** Define usability test scenarios, validate accessibility compliance

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release planning, orchestrate deployments, and ensure smooth transitions from development to production. They own the release process and serve as the point of coordination for all teams involved in go-live activities.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain release plans and deployment schedules
- Coordinate across teams to ensure release readiness (dev, QA, operations, support)
- Manage release notes and communication
- Orchestrate deployment execution
- Monitor post-deployment health and incident response
- Document rollback procedures and execute rollbacks if needed
- Conduct post-release retrospectives
- Track release metrics and process improvements

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk through consistent, repeatable processes
- Enable frequent, reliable releases to production
- Minimize customer impact during deployments
- Build confidence in the release process

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings with cross-functional teams
- Pre-release readiness checklists and sign-offs
- Release notes and customer announcements
- Deployment day coordination and status updates
- Post-deployment incident communications
- Release metrics and trend reporting

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With QA Lead:** Conduct smoke testing, validate deployment procedures, confirm post-deployment functionality
- **With Developers:** Coordinate code freeze dates, manage release branches and tags
- **With Project Manager:** Align release dates with project milestones, escalate deployment risks
- **With Operations/Ops:** Coordinate infrastructure changes, manage deployment environments
- **With Stakeholder/Sponsor:** Report on release status, communicate customer impact, manage launch announcements
